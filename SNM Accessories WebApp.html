About SNM Accessories

Our Journey & Core Philosophy

Committed to uncompromising quality, SNM Accessories has evolved into a premier manufacturer and global supplier of ready-made garment accessories.

Precision Manufacturing Meets Global Standards

SNM Accessories started journey in 2011. Through continuous technological investment, strict quality control, and an unwavering commitment to excellence, we have expanded our portfolio to encompass a comprehensive suite of garment trims and accessories.

Today, our advanced manufacturing facility houses cutting-edge machinery for woven and printed labels, hangtags, drawstrings, elastics, jacquard elastics, industrial threads, buttons, zippers, and retail packaging solutions.
